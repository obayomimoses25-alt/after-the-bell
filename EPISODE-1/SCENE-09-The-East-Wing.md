# AFTER THE BELL
## EPISODE 1 — SCENE 9

### THE EAST WING

**TIME:** Approximately 60 seconds

---

The classroom empties.

Leon walks toward the East Wing.

Jordan catches up.

**JORDAN:**

"You're not actually going there, are you?"

**LEON:**

"I just want to look."

**JORDAN:**

"That's exactly how people get into trouble."

Lucas follows them.

**LUCAS:**

"I'm coming."

They reach a quieter section of the school.

The sound of students becomes distant.

Ahead is a hallway marked:

EAST WING — AUTHORIZED PERSONNEL ONLY

Jordan stops.

**JORDAN:**

"Okay. That's enough."

Leon looks at the sign.

Then at the note.

He notices a security camera above them.

Its small red indicator is glowing.

Leon looks directly at it.

Then back at Jordan.

**LEON:**

"We're already here."

They continue.

The architecture becomes older.

Wooden walls.

Historic portraits.

Old display cases.

Leon notices a photograph.

He stops.

The plaque beneath it carries Maya's name.

Leon looks closer.

---

**END OF SCENE 9**
