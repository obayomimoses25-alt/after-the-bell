# AFTER THE BELL
## EPISODE 1 — SCENE 7

### THE EMPTY DESK

**TIME:** Approximately 50 seconds

---

The lesson begins.

Students take notes while the teacher writes on the board.

Leon tries to concentrate.

His eyes wander across the classroom.

Near the windows is an empty desk.

A small name card sits on it:

MAYA BENNETT.

Leon stares at it.

Jordan notices.

He leans toward Leon.

**JORDAN:**

"Stop looking."

**LEON:**

"Why?"

Jordan glances toward the teacher.

**JORDAN:**

"People here don't like questions about her."

Leon looks back at the empty desk.

He notices faint marks carved into its surface.

He reaches toward it.

Before he can touch it—

**TEACHER:**

"Leon."

Leon immediately pulls his hand back.

**LEON:**

"Yes, ma'am?"

**TEACHER:**

"Eyes forward."

Leon nods.

The camera lingers briefly on Maya's empty desk.

---

**END OF SCENE 7**
