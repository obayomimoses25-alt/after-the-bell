# AFTER THE BELL
## EPISODE 1 — SCENE 13

### THE YEARBOOK

**TIME:** Approximately 60 seconds

---

Leon searches the library shelves.

He finds the previous year's school yearbook.

The three boys sit together.

Leon flips through pages.

Sports.

Academic competitions.

School events.

Awards.

Then he reaches the academic achievements section.

Maya's photograph appears.

Underneath:

MAYA BENNETT — NATIONAL ACADEMIC FINALIST

Lucas looks impressed.

**LUCAS:**

"She was really good."

Leon continues examining the page.

Something is wrong.

A photograph has been removed.

Only a faint rectangular mark remains.

Jordan notices.

**JORDAN:**

"Someone took it."

Leon nods.

**LEON:**

"Recently."

He closes the book.

Then opens it again.

A small piece of paper is caught between the pages.

Leon slowly pulls it out.

---

**END OF SCENE 13**
