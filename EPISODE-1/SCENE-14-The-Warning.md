# AFTER THE BELL
## EPISODE 1 — SCENE 14

### THE WARNING

**TIME:** Approximately 60 seconds

---

Before Leon can read the paper, Emma approaches their table.

She is confident but cautious.

She looks at the yearbook.

**EMMA:**

"You're looking for Maya."

Leon looks at her.

**LEON:**

"You knew her?"

Emma sits opposite them.

**EMMA:**

"Everyone knew Maya."

**LEON:**

"What happened to her?"

Emma looks around the library.

**EMMA:**

"She found something."

Leon leans forward.

**LEON:**

"What?"

Emma hesitates.

**EMMA:**

"I don't know exactly."

Jordan looks suspicious.

**JORDAN:**

"Then why are you telling us?"

Emma closes the yearbook.

**EMMA:**

"Because you're already looking."

She stands.

**EMMA:**

"Stop before they notice."

She walks away.

Lucas watches her.

**LUCAS:**

"Who are 'they'?"

Leon looks at the paper he found.

He doesn't answer.

---

**END OF SCENE 14**
