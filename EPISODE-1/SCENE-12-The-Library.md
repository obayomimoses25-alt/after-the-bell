# AFTER THE BELL
## EPISODE 1 — SCENE 12

### THE LIBRARY

**TIME:** Approximately 55 seconds

---

The boys return to the main part of the school.

They enter the grand library.

Students study at long wooden tables.

Sunlight fills the room.

Leon places the note on a table.

**LEON:**

"There's something we're missing."

Jordan looks at it.

**JORDAN:**

"Maybe we should leave it alone."

**LEON:**

"Then why hide it?"

Lucas looks at the note.

**LUCAS:**

"East Wing. After the bell."

Leon thinks.

**LEON:**

"Maybe Maya left it."

Jordan looks at him.

**JORDAN:**

"Then find out what happened to her first."

Leon looks toward the library shelves.

**LEON:**

"That's what I'm going to do."

**END OF SCENE 12**
