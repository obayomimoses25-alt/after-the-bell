# AFTER THE BELL
## EPISODE 1 — SCENE 10

### THE PHOTOGRAPH

**TIME:** Approximately 60 seconds

---

The photograph shows Maya standing with several students during an academic competition.

She looks confident and happy.

Leon studies it.

One student's face has been deliberately scratched out.

**LEON:**

"Why would someone do that?"

Jordan looks closer.

**JORDAN:**

"I don't know."

Lucas examines the plaque.

**LUCAS:**

"This picture is from last year."

A sound comes from farther down the corridor.

A door closing.

The three boys turn.

Nobody is there.

Leon looks toward an old door.

A thin line of light is visible beneath it.

He walks toward it.

Jordan reaches for his shoulder.

**JORDAN:**

"Leon."

Leon stops.

From inside the room comes the sound of papers being moved.

Then a voice.

**DAVID:**

"Someone's been asking questions."

The boys freeze.

**DAVID:**

"And I think I know who."

Leon looks at Jordan.

Jordan's eyes widen.

Leon slowly steps backward.

---

**END OF SCENE 10**
