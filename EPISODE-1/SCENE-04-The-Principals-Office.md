# AFTER THE BELL
## EPISODE 1 — SCENE 4

### THE PRINCIPAL'S OFFICE

**TIME:** Approximately 65 seconds

---

The teacher opens the door to an elegant principal's office.

Leon enters.

The room reflects the school's wealth and history.

A large wooden desk sits near the window.

Academic awards line the walls.

Old photographs show generations of successful students.

The PRINCIPAL sits behind the desk.

The teacher stands beside Leon.

**TEACHER:**

"He threw a folded paper across the classroom."

The principal looks directly at Leon.

**PRINCIPAL:**

"Did you?"

Leon doesn't try to deny it.

**LEON:**

"Yes, sir."

The principal studies him for a moment.

**PRINCIPAL:**

"Why?"

Leon gives a small shrug.

**LEON:**

"I thought it would be funny."

The principal remains serious.

**PRINCIPAL:**

"Don't let it happen again."

Leon nods.

**LEON:**

"It won't."

The teacher gives Leon a final look and leaves the office.

Leon turns toward the door.

Before he can leave, the door opens.

David enters.

He is carrying the same examination folder Leon saw in the corridor.

David walks directly to the principal's desk.

Leon notices the folder.

David places it on the desk.

The principal immediately becomes more serious.

David opens the folder.

Inside are confidential student examination records.

Leon accidentally catches a glimpse.

Several names appear.

Then one name catches his attention:

MAYA.

Leon stares for a moment.

The principal notices.

He immediately closes the folder.

Leon looks at him.

A brief silence.

---

**END OF SCENE 4**
