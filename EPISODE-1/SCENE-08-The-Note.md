# AFTER THE BELL
## EPISODE 1 — SCENE 8

### THE NOTE

**TIME:** Approximately 55 seconds

---

Later, students work on an assignment.

Leon notices something underneath Maya's desk.

A small folded piece of paper.

He looks around.

Nobody appears to be watching.

He quietly picks it up.

He unfolds it.

Written across the paper:

"AFTER THE BELL. EAST WING."

Leon reads it again.

Jordan notices.

**JORDAN:**

"What is it?"

Leon shows him.

Jordan's expression changes.

**JORDAN:**

"Put that back."

**LEON:**

"Why?"

**JORDAN:**

"Because whoever wrote it didn't want everyone seeing it."

Lucas looks over.

**LUCAS:**

"What are you two whispering about?"

Leon folds the note and slips it into his notebook.

**LEON:**

"Nothing."

Lucas gives him a suspicious look.

The teacher announces that class is ending.

Students begin packing their bags.

Leon looks at the clock.

Then at the note.

The bell rings.

Leon stands.

---

**END OF SCENE 8**
