# AFTER THE BELL
## EPISODE 1 — SCENE 1

### THE PERFECT MORNING

**TIME:** Approximately 50 seconds

---

Open immediately with a visually stunning and energetic shot.

Do not use a slow fade-in.

A luxury black car smoothly turns through the enormous gates of an elite American private boarding school. The camera moves dynamically alongside it as other expensive cars arrive behind it.

Students in sophisticated school uniforms cross the beautiful courtyard. Some walk in groups, some greet friends, while others hurry toward the main building.

The school looks magnificent: historic stone architecture, enormous windows, manicured lawns, elegant walkways and a grand staircase leading to the entrance.

The atmosphere is youthful, energetic and sophisticated.

The camera moves through the crowd and follows students entering the building.

**NARRATOR:**

"Every morning, Westbridge Academy looked perfect."

Show the impressive interior.

Students move through polished corridors lined with trophies, framed photographs, academic awards and portraits of former graduating classes.

**NARRATOR:**

"Perfect grades. Perfect families. Perfect reputations."

The camera continues down the corridor and enters a classroom.

Students settle into their seats.

At the back sits LEON, an intelligent, confident teenage student with a naturally observant expression.

Beside him are his friends JORDAN and LUCAS.

The classroom is bright, elegant and full of quiet morning energy.

Leon looks around.

Then he reaches beneath his desk.

**END OF SCENE 1**
