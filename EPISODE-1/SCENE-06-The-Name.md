# AFTER THE BELL
## EPISODE 1 — SCENE 6

### THE NAME

**TIME:** Approximately 50 seconds

---

The corridor slowly returns to normal after the bell.

Students move between classrooms while Leon, Jordan and Lucas remain together.

Jordan looks at Leon.

**JORDAN:**

"Maya?"

Leon nods.

**LEON:**

"That was the name I saw."

Lucas looks at him.

**LUCAS:**

"You mean Maya Bennett?"

Leon turns.

**LEON:**

"You know her?"

Lucas hesitates.

**LUCAS:**

"Everybody knows her."

Jordan becomes serious.

**JORDAN:**

"She disappeared from school last semester."

Leon looks surprised.

**LEON:**

"Disappeared?"

Lucas lowers his voice.

**LUCAS:**

"That's what people say."

Leon looks toward the administration wing.

**LEON:**

"Then why would her examination records still be there?"

Neither boy answers.

The bell rings.

The three enter the classroom.

Leon looks back once toward the administration corridor.

---

**END OF SCENE 6**
