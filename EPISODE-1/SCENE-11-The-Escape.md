# AFTER THE BELL
## EPISODE 1 — SCENE 11

### THE ESCAPE

**TIME:** Approximately 55 seconds

---

The boys quickly move away from the door.

They try not to run.

Leon keeps looking behind him.

The door opens.

David steps into the corridor.

He looks around.

The boys hide behind a large decorative pillar.

Jordan whispers:

**JORDAN:**

"Don't move."

David walks toward them.

His footsteps echo.

He stops.

Leon remains completely still.

David looks toward the pillar.

A long pause.

Then the distant school bell rings.

David turns away and continues walking.

The boys wait.

Lucas finally whispers:

**LUCAS:**

"Okay. That was close."

Leon looks toward David.

**LEON:**

"He knows someone is asking questions."

Jordan looks at him.

**JORDAN:**

"And now he might know it's us."

Leon looks at the note in his hand.

---

**END OF SCENE 11**
