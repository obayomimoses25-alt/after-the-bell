# AFTER THE BELL
## EPISODE 1 — SCENE 15

### THE SECOND CLUE

**TIME:** Approximately 65 seconds

---

Leon unfolds the paper.

Written across it:

"THE BELL HIDES WHAT THE SCHOOL CANNOT."

Jordan reads it.

**JORDAN:**

"What does that mean?"

Leon looks toward the enormous clock above the library entrance.

The second hand moves.

Tick.

Tick.

Tick.

Leon thinks.

**LEON:**

"Every time the bell rings, everyone moves."

Jordan listens.

**LEON:**

"Classrooms empty. Corridors fill."

Lucas looks at the clock.

**LUCAS:**

"So?"

Leon looks toward the East Wing.

**LEON:**

"Maybe that's when something happens."

The school bell rings.

Students immediately begin leaving the library.

Leon looks toward the East Wing.

A door in the distance quietly opens.

Leon closes his notebook.

**LEON:**

"After the bell."

He walks toward the corridor.

Jordan and Lucas exchange a look.

Then follow.

The camera pulls back, revealing the enormous library filled with moving students.

The beautiful school continues operating normally.

But somewhere beyond the crowd, a mysterious door remains open.

CUT TO BLACK.

---

**END OF SCENE 15**
