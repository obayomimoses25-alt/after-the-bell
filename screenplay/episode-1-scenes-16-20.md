# AFTER THE BELL
## EPISODE 1 — SCENES 16–20

---

## CONTINUITY & VISUAL STYLE

Continue directly from Scenes 11–15.

Maintain the same mature, sophisticated stylized 3D cinematic storytelling style. Westbridge Academy is a wealthy, prestigious American private boarding school with beautiful architecture, elegant interiors and a highly polished atmosphere.

Keep Leon, Jordan, Lucas, Emma, David, Maya and all established character appearances consistent.

The sequence should feel lively, intelligent, mysterious and exciting. Avoid comedy, horror and overly gloomy visuals. Suspense should come from discovery and unanswered questions.

---

## SCENE 16 — AFTER THE BELL

**TIME:** Approximately 55 seconds

The library empties rapidly after the bell.

Students stream into the corridors.

Leon, Jordan and Lucas move with the crowd.

Leon keeps looking toward the East Wing.

Jordan notices.

**JORDAN:**

"You're actually going back?"

**LEON:**

"Just for a minute."

**JORDAN:**

"That is exactly what you said last time."

Lucas follows them.

**LUCAS:**

"And somehow we're still following him."

They reach the entrance to the East Wing.

The crowd becomes thinner.

The sound of students fades behind them.

Leon looks at the handwritten card again.

"THE BELL HIDES WHAT THE SCHOOL CANNOT."

A second bell sounds in the distance.

Leon looks down the corridor.

A door at the far end is slightly open.

---

## SCENE 17 — THE OPEN DOOR

**TIME:** Approximately 60 seconds

The three boys walk slowly toward the open door.

Sunlight from the corridor windows creates long shadows across the polished floor.

Leon reaches the doorway.

Inside is a small, old administrative room.

Shelves contain archived school documents.

Boxes are stacked neatly against the wall.

The room appears abandoned.

Lucas looks around.

**LUCAS:**

"This place hasn't been used in years."

Leon notices a desk.

A small school archive box sits on it.

The label reads:

**MAYA BENNETT — ACADEMIC FILE**

Leon stops.

Jordan looks at him.

**JORDAN:**

"That's impossible."

Leon reaches toward the box.

Before he can touch it—

Footsteps echo from the corridor.

The boys turn.

Someone is approaching.

Leon quickly closes the door.

They move behind a tall cabinet inside the room.

The footsteps stop outside.

A shadow appears beneath the door.

Nobody speaks.

After several seconds, the footsteps continue away.

Leon slowly looks at the archive box again.

---

## SCENE 18 — MAYA'S FILE

**TIME:** Approximately 65 seconds

Leon carefully picks up the archive box.

They place it on the desk.

He opens it.

Inside are academic reports, competition records and school correspondence.

Everything looks ordinary at first.

Then Leon finds a document with a red stamp:

**TRANSFERRED — CONFIDENTIAL**

Jordan reads it.

**JORDAN:**

"Transferred where?"

Leon searches through the papers.

There is no answer.

Lucas notices another document.

**LUCAS:**

"Look."

It is a disciplinary report.

Maya's name appears at the top.

But the details have been blacked out.

Leon turns the page.

At the bottom is a signature.

**DAVID HARPER.**

Leon looks at Jordan.

**LEON:**

"David."

Jordan becomes increasingly concerned.

**JORDAN:**

"Maybe we should put everything back."

Leon continues searching.

He finds a small photograph.

It shows Maya standing beside three other students.

One of them is the same student whose face was scratched out of the old school photograph.

Leon stares at him.

**LEON:**

"Who is that?"

---

## SCENE 19 — THE FOUR STUDENTS

**TIME:** Approximately 60 seconds

Leon holds the photograph closer to the light.

The four students stand outside the school several months earlier.

Maya is smiling.

Beside her are three students.

One face has been deliberately crossed out with a black marker.

Jordan examines the photograph.

**JORDAN:**

"Maybe that's who they don't want anyone remembering."

Lucas looks at the back of the photograph.

There is handwriting.

Leon turns it over.

Written on the back:

**"WE ALL SAW IT."**

Below the sentence are four initials.

**M.B. — E.R. — J.C. — A.S.**

Leon reads them.

**LEON:**

"Maya... and three others."

Jordan points to the initials.

**JORDAN:**

"E.R."

Leon looks at him.

**LEON:**

"Who's E.R.?"

Lucas suddenly remembers something.

**LUCAS:**

"Elias Reed."

Leon looks up.

**LEON:**

"You know him?"

Lucas shakes his head.

**LUCAS:**

"Not personally."

Jordan looks toward the door.

**JORDAN:**

"Guys..."

The boys turn.

The door handle is slowly moving.

Someone is outside.

---

## SCENE 20 — CAUGHT

**TIME:** Approximately 70 seconds

Leon quickly puts the photograph and documents back into the archive box.

The door begins opening.

The boys look for somewhere to hide.

Leon notices another door at the back of the room.

He opens it.

It leads into a narrow service corridor.

**LEON:**

"Go."

Jordan and Lucas slip through.

Leon follows.

The door behind them opens.

David enters the archive room.

He immediately notices the archive box.

He walks toward it.

He opens the lid.

His expression changes.

He realizes someone has been looking through Maya's file.

Meanwhile, the boys move quickly through the service corridor.

They emerge into another part of the school.

Jordan catches his breath.

**JORDAN:**

"We need to stop."

Leon looks at him.

**LEON:**

"Did you see that photograph?"

**JORDAN:**

"Yes."

**LEON:**

"Someone erased one of those students."

Lucas looks at Leon.

**LUCAS:**

"And now someone knows we found the file."

Leon looks back toward the corridor.

**LEON:**

"Then we're getting closer."

Cut back to the archive room.

David stands alone beside Maya's open file.

He removes his phone.

**DAVID:**

"They found it."

He listens.

His expression becomes serious.

**DAVID:**

"Yes. Leon."

A pause.

David looks toward the door the boys escaped through.

**DAVID:**

"I'll handle it."

**CUT TO BLACK.**
