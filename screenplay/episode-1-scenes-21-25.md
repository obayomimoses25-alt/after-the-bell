## SCENE 21 — THE WARNING

**TIME:** Approximately 55 seconds

Leon, Jordan and Lucas return to the main corridor.

Students move around them, completely unaware of what just happened.

Leon looks back toward the East Wing.

Jordan notices.

**JORDAN:**

"We shouldn't have gone in there."

**LEON:**

"Maybe."

**JORDAN:**

"Leon, I'm serious."

Leon looks at him.

**LEON:**

"So am I."

Lucas looks between them.

**LUCAS:**

"Can we at least agree not to break into another room today?"

Leon almost smiles.

Before he can answer, Emma appears ahead of them.

She walks directly toward Leon.

Her expression is serious.

**EMMA:**

"You went to the East Wing."

Leon stops.

**LEON:**

"How do you know?"

Emma looks around.

**EMMA:**

"Because someone saw you."

Jordan looks concerned.

**JORDAN:**

"Who?"

Emma doesn't answer immediately.

**EMMA:**

"David."

Leon looks toward the administration wing.

---

## SCENE 22 — EMMA'S SECRET

**TIME:** Approximately 60 seconds

Emma leads them toward a quieter staircase.

They stop where they can speak privately.

**LEON:**

"You said Maya found something."

Emma nods.

**EMMA:**

"She wasn't the only one."

Leon waits.

**EMMA:**

"There were four students."

She counts them quietly.

**EMMA:**

"Maya. Elias. Rachel. And Andrew."

Lucas recognizes the names.

**LUCAS:**

"They were the top students in the school."

Emma nods.

**EMMA:**

"They discovered something connected to the examination system."

Jordan looks confused.

**JORDAN:**

"What kind of something?"

Emma shakes her head.

**EMMA:**

"I never found out."

**LEON:**

"Then why did Maya disappear?"

Emma looks directly at him.

**EMMA:**

"She refused to stay quiet."

A silence follows.

The sound of students laughing in the distant courtyard contrasts with the serious conversation.

**LEON:**

"What happened to the other three?"

Emma looks away.

**EMMA:**

"That's the part nobody talks about."

---

## SCENE 23 — ELIAS REED

**TIME:** Approximately 55 seconds

The boys return to the library.

Leon immediately searches the school's digital student archive on a computer.

He types:

**ELIAS REED**

A student profile appears.

Academic awards.

Debate champion.

National scholarship finalist.

Then Leon notices something strange.

The profile ends abruptly.

No graduation information.

No transfer information.

No explanation.

**LEON:**

"He just disappears."

Jordan looks over.

**JORDAN:**

"Maybe he transferred."

Leon searches.

**LEON:**

"There's no transfer record."

Lucas looks at the screen.

**LUCAS:**

"Try Rachel."

Leon searches.

Another incomplete profile appears.

Rachel's records also stop suddenly.

Leon searches Andrew.

The same thing.

Leon leans back.

**LEON:**

"All three."

Jordan looks increasingly uneasy.

**JORDAN:**

"And Maya."

Leon nods.

Four students.

Four incomplete records.

---

## SCENE 24 — THE MESSAGE

**TIME:** Approximately 65 seconds

Leon closes the student archive.

A notification suddenly appears on the computer.

**UNKNOWN USER:**

**STOP SEARCHING.**

Leon stares at the screen.

Jordan steps closer.

**JORDAN:**

"Did you get that?"

The message disappears.

A second message appears.

**"YOU DON'T KNOW WHAT YOU'RE LOOKING FOR."**

Lucas looks around the library.

**LUCAS:**

"Someone's watching us."

Leon tries to identify the account.

Nothing.

The computer suddenly returns to the normal library homepage.

Leon looks at the screen.

**LEON:**

"They're inside the school network."

Jordan looks at him.

**JORDAN:**

"So what do we do?"

Leon closes the laptop.

**LEON:**

"Find out who sent it."

---

## SCENE 25 — THE LOCKER

**TIME:** Approximately 70 seconds

Later that afternoon, students move through the main hallway.

Leon walks past a row of old student lockers.

He notices one with a faded nameplate.

**MAYA BENNETT.**

Leon stops.

The locker is locked.

Jordan approaches.

**JORDAN:**

"Leon."

Leon looks at him.

**LEON:**

"Think about it."

He looks around.

Nobody seems to be watching.

Lucas joins them.

Leon notices a small number written beneath the locker:

**217.**

He remembers something from the photograph.

The back of the photo had four initials.

**M.B.**

**E.R.**

**J.C.**

**A.S.**

Leon looks at the number again.

**LEON:**

"Two-one-seven."

Jordan doesn't understand.

**JORDAN:**

"What about it?"

Leon looks toward the old school clock.

Its hands point toward two seventeen.

A distant bell begins to ring.

Leon looks back at Maya's locker.

The lock clicks.

The locker door opens slightly by itself.

The three boys freeze.

Inside is a single envelope.

Written across it:

**FOR THE PERSON WHO FINDS THIS.**

Leon slowly reaches toward it.

Before he can touch it—

A voice comes from behind them.

**DAVID:**

"I wouldn't do that."

The boys turn.

David stands at the end of the hallway.

Watching them.

**CUT TO BLACK.**
