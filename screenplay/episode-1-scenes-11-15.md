# AFTER THE BELL
## EPISODE 1 — SCENES 11–15

---

## CONTINUITY & VISUAL STYLE

Continue immediately from Scenes 6–10.

Maintain the mature, sophisticated stylized 3D cinematic look. Westbridge Academy remains a wealthy, prestigious American private boarding school.

Keep all character designs, clothing, locations and personalities consistent.

The sequence should feel energetic and intriguing rather than depressing. Build suspense through discoveries, conversations and visual clues rather than horror.

## SCENE 11 — THE ESCAPE

**TIME:** Approximately 55 seconds

The boys move quickly down the East Wing corridor.

They try not to run.

Leon keeps looking over his shoulder.

Behind them, the door they were standing near slowly opens.

David steps into the corridor.

He looks left.

Then right.

The boys disappear around a corner.

David walks toward the corridor.

Leon, Jordan and Lucas hide behind a large decorative pillar.

Jordan whispers.

**JORDAN:**

"Don't move."

David walks past them.

His footsteps echo through the corridor.

He stops.

Leon holds his breath.

David looks toward the pillar.

A long pause.

Then a distant bell rings.

David turns and walks away.

The boys wait until he disappears.

Lucas exhales.

**LUCAS:**

"Okay. That was close."

Leon looks toward the direction David went.

**LEON:**

"He knows someone is asking questions."

Jordan looks at him.

**JORDAN:**

"And now he might know it's us."

Leon looks at the folded note in his hand.

---

## SCENE 12 — THE LIBRARY

**TIME:** Approximately 55 seconds

The boys return to the main part of the school and enter the grand library.

The atmosphere becomes lively again.

Students study at long wooden tables.

Sunlight pours through tall windows.

Leon sits with Jordan and Lucas at a quiet table.

He places the note on the table.

**LEON:**

"There's something we're missing."

Jordan looks at the note.

**JORDAN:**

"Maybe we should leave it alone."

**LEON:**

"Then why hide it?"

Lucas points at the note.

**LUCAS:**

"East Wing. After the bell."

Leon thinks.

**LEON:**

"Maybe Maya left it."

Jordan looks around the library.

**JORDAN:**

"Then find out what happened to her first."

Leon looks toward the library shelves.

**LEON:**

"That's what I'm going to do."

---

## SCENE 13 — THE YEARBOOK

**TIME:** Approximately 65 seconds

Leon walks through the library shelves.

He searches through old school yearbooks.

Jordan and Lucas follow.

Leon finds the previous year's yearbook.

They sit at a table.

Leon flips through pages.

Sports.

Academic competitions.

School events.

Graduation photographs.

Then he reaches the section featuring academic achievements.

Maya's photograph appears.

Underneath it:

**MAYA BENNETT — NATIONAL ACADEMIC FINALIST**

Leon studies the photograph.

Lucas looks over his shoulder.

**LUCAS:**

"She was really good."

Jordan points to the page.

**JORDAN:**

"Look at the date."

Leon notices something strange.

The page has been altered.

A photograph has been removed.

There is a faint rectangular mark where another photograph used to be.

Leon closes the book.

**LEON:**

"Someone removed something."

Jordan looks concerned.

**JORDAN:**

"Why?"

Before Leon can answer, someone approaches their table.

---

## SCENE 14 — THE WARNING

**TIME:** Approximately 60 seconds

The boys look up.

It is **EMMA**, a confident student who has clearly been listening.

She looks at the yearbook.

**EMMA:**

"You're looking for Maya."

Leon looks at Jordan.

Then back at Emma.

**LEON:**

"You knew her?"

Emma sits opposite them.

**EMMA:**

"Everyone knew Maya."

**LEON:**

"What happened to her?"

Emma hesitates.

She looks around to make sure nobody is listening.

**EMMA:**

"She found something."

Leon leans slightly forward.

**LEON:**

"What?"

Emma shakes her head.

**EMMA:**

"I don't know exactly."

A pause.

**EMMA:**

"But after she found it, everything changed."

Jordan looks uneasy.

**JORDAN:**

"Why are you telling us this?"

Emma closes the yearbook.

**EMMA:**

"Because you're already looking."

She stands.

Before leaving, she looks directly at Leon.

**EMMA:**

"Stop before they notice."

She walks away.

Leon watches her.

Lucas looks confused.

**LUCAS:**

"Who are 'they'?"

Leon doesn't answer.

---

## SCENE 15 — THE SECOND CLUE

**TIME:** Approximately 65 seconds

The boys remain at the table.

Leon opens the yearbook again.

He examines the empty space where the photograph was removed.

Something is caught between two pages.

A tiny folded card.

Leon carefully pulls it out.

He unfolds it.

Inside is a handwritten message:

**"THE BELL HIDES WHAT THE SCHOOL CANNOT."**

Leon reads it silently.

Jordan looks at him.

**JORDAN:**

"What does that mean?"

Leon looks toward the enormous clock hanging above the library entrance.

The second hand moves.

Tick.

Tick.

Tick.

Lucas looks at the clock.

**LUCAS:**

"The bell?"

Leon slowly realizes something.

**LEON:**

"Every time the bell rings, everyone moves."

Jordan looks at him.

**LEON:**

"Classrooms empty. Corridors fill."

A pause.

**LEON:**

"Maybe that's when something happens."

Suddenly—

The school bell rings.

Students immediately begin leaving the library.

Leon looks at the clock.

Then toward the East Wing.

His expression changes.

**LEON:**

"After the bell."

The camera follows Leon's eyes toward the distant East Wing.

The hallway is crowded.

But somewhere beyond the crowd, a door quietly opens.

**CUT TO BLACK.**
