# AFTER THE BELL
## EPISODE 1 — SCENES 6–10

---

## CONTINUITY & VISUAL STYLE

Continue directly from Scenes 1–5.

Maintain the same mature, sophisticated stylized 3D cinematic look. The setting remains Westbridge Academy, an elite American private boarding school.

Keep Leon, Jordan, Lucas, David, the Principal, and all established character appearances consistent.

The atmosphere is lively and sophisticated, but the mystery is beginning to intensify. Avoid comedy, horror, excessive darkness, or sadness. Every scene should feel visually interesting and purposeful.

---

## SCENE 6 — THE NAME

**TIME:** Approximately 50 seconds

The corridor slowly returns to normal after the bell.

Students move toward their classrooms.

Leon remains standing with Jordan and Lucas.

Jordan looks at Leon.

**JORDAN:**

"Maya?"

Leon nods.

**LEON:**

"That was the name I saw."

Lucas looks around before speaking.

**LUCAS:**

"You mean Maya Bennett?"

Leon looks at him.

**LEON:**

"You know her?"

Lucas hesitates.

**LUCAS:**

"Everybody knows her."

Jordan becomes more serious.

**JORDAN:**

"She disappeared from school last semester."

Leon looks surprised.

**LEON:**

"Disappeared?"

Lucas quickly lowers his voice.

**LUCAS:**

"That's what people say."

A group of students walks past them.

Leon watches them disappear into the classroom.

**LEON:**

"Then why would her examination records still be here?"

Jordan doesn't answer.

The bell rings again.

The three boys enter the classroom.

Leon looks back once toward the administration wing.

---

## SCENE 7 — THE EMPTY DESK

**TIME:** Approximately 55 seconds

The boys return to their classroom.

The teacher begins the lesson.

Leon tries to concentrate, but his attention keeps drifting.

He looks across the room.

There is an empty desk near the windows.

A small name card sits on it.

**MAYA BENNETT.**

Leon stares.

Jordan notices.

He leans toward Leon.

**JORDAN:**

"Stop looking."

**LEON:**

"Why?"

Jordan glances toward the teacher.

**JORDAN:**

"Because people here don't like questions about her."

Leon looks back at the empty desk.

There are faint scratches on the wooden surface, as though someone repeatedly wrote something there.

Leon quietly reaches toward it.

Before he can touch it—

**TEACHER:**

"Leon."

He immediately pulls his hand back.

The class looks at him.

**LEON:**

"Yes, ma'am?"

**TEACHER:**

"Eyes forward."

Leon nods.

The camera lingers briefly on Maya's empty desk.

---

## SCENE 8 — THE NOTE

**TIME:** Approximately 60 seconds

During a short classroom activity, students move around the room.

Leon notices something underneath Maya's desk.

A small folded piece of paper.

He looks around.

Nobody is watching.

Leon bends down and quietly picks it up.

He unfolds it.

There is only one handwritten sentence:

**"AFTER THE BELL. EAST WING."**

Leon reads it twice.

His expression changes.

Jordan notices.

**JORDAN:**

"What is it?"

Leon quietly shows him.

Jordan reads it.

**JORDAN:**

"Put that back."

**LEON:**

"Why?"

**JORDAN:**

"Because whoever wrote it didn't want everyone seeing it."

Lucas looks over.

**LUCAS:**

"What are you two whispering about?"

Leon folds the note and slips it into his notebook.

**LEON:**

"Nothing."

Lucas gives him a suspicious look.

The teacher announces that the class is over.

Students begin packing their bags.

Leon looks toward the clock.

Then toward the corridor.

The final bell for the period rings.

Leon remembers the note.

---

## SCENE 9 — THE EAST WING

**TIME:** Approximately 65 seconds

After class, students leave the classroom.

Jordan catches up with Leon.

**JORDAN:**

"You're not actually going there, are you?"

Leon continues walking.

**LEON:**

"I just want to look."

**JORDAN:**

"That's exactly how people get into trouble."

Lucas follows them.

**LUCAS:**

"I'm coming."

They move through a quieter section of the school.

The lively corridors gradually become empty.

They reach a hallway marked:

**EAST WING — AUTHORIZED PERSONNEL ONLY**

Leon looks at the sign.

Jordan stops.

**JORDAN:**

"Okay. That's enough."

Leon notices an old security camera above them.

The small red indicator light is on.

He looks directly at it.

Then back at Jordan.

**LEON:**

"We're already here."

The three continue carefully down the corridor.

The architecture becomes older.

The polished modern school gives way to older wooden doors and framed photographs.

Leon notices a photograph on the wall.

It shows a group of students from years earlier.

Maya's name appears on a small plaque beneath another photograph nearby.

Leon stops.

---

## SCENE 10 — THE PHOTOGRAPH

**TIME:** Approximately 70 seconds

Leon steps closer to the photograph.

It shows Maya standing with several students during an academic competition.

She looks confident and happy.

But something catches Leon's attention.

One student standing beside Maya has been carefully scratched out of the photograph.

Leon leans closer.

**LEON:**

"Why would someone do that?"

Jordan looks at the photograph.

**JORDAN:**

"I don't know."

Lucas examines the plaque beneath it.

**LUCAS:**

"This picture is from last year."

Leon looks at Maya.

Then at the scratched-out student.

A faint sound comes from farther down the corridor.

A door closing.

The three boys turn.

Nobody is there.

Leon looks down.

A thin line of light is visible beneath one of the old doors.

He walks toward it.

Jordan quietly grabs his shoulder.

**JORDAN:**

"Leon."

Leon stops.

From inside the room, they hear papers being moved.

Then a voice.

**DAVID:**

"Someone's been asking questions."

The boys freeze.

Leon looks at Jordan.

Jordan's eyes widen.

David continues speaking to someone inside.

**DAVID:**

"And I think I know who."

Leon slowly steps backward.

The boys quietly retreat down the corridor.

The camera remains on the closed door.

A moment later, the handle begins to move.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

**END OF SCENES 6–10**
