# AFTER THE BELL
## EPISODE 1 — SCENES 1–5

---

## OVERALL VISUAL STYLE

Create a mature, sophisticated, stylized 3D cinematic storytelling series set in a prestigious American private boarding school.

The animation should feel premium, detailed and believable, with natural human proportions, realistic environments, sophisticated clothing, expressive facial reactions and cinematic camera movement.

Do not make it childish, goofy, exaggerated, anime-style, or like a basic cartoon.

The atmosphere should be lively and attractive, with an underlying mystery. The school is wealthy, prestigious and beautiful, but there should be subtle hints that something is hidden beneath its perfect appearance.

Maintain consistent character appearances throughout all scenes.

---

## SCENE 1 — THE PERFECT MORNING

**TIME:** Approximately 50 seconds

Open immediately with a visually stunning and energetic shot.

Do not use a slow fade-in.

A luxury black car smoothly turns through the enormous gates of an elite American private boarding school. The camera moves dynamically alongside it as other expensive cars arrive behind it.

Students in sophisticated school uniforms cross the beautiful courtyard. Some walk in groups, some greet friends, while others hurry toward the main building.

The school looks magnificent: historic stone architecture, enormous windows, manicured lawns, elegant walkways and a grand staircase leading to the entrance.

The atmosphere is youthful, energetic and sophisticated.

The camera moves through the crowd and follows students entering the building.

**NARRATOR:**

"Every morning, Westbridge Academy looked perfect."

Show the impressive interior.

Students move through polished corridors lined with trophies, framed photographs, academic awards and portraits of former graduating classes.

**NARRATOR:**

"Perfect grades. Perfect families. Perfect reputations."

The camera continues down the corridor and enters a classroom.

Students settle into their seats.

At the back sits **LEON**, an intelligent, confident teenage student with a naturally observant expression.

Beside him are his friends **JORDAN** and **LUCAS**.

The classroom is bright, elegant and full of quiet morning energy.

Leon looks around.

Then he reaches beneath his desk.

---

## SCENE 2 — THE PAPER

**TIME:** Approximately 55 seconds

Leon quietly takes a small piece of paper and begins folding it.

Jordan notices.

He looks at Leon with immediate suspicion.

**JORDAN:**

"Don't."

Leon looks at him and gives a small, confident smile.

**LEON:**

"Relax."

Lucas turns toward them.

**LUCAS:**

"That usually means trouble."

Leon suppresses a laugh.

He looks toward the classroom door.

The teacher still hasn't arrived.

Leon finishes folding the paper.

He holds it between his fingers.

The camera briefly follows his eyes toward the empty doorway.

Then back to the paper.

Leon flicks it across the classroom.

The paper flies through the air.

At that exact moment, the classroom door opens.

The **TEACHER** walks inside.

The paper lightly strikes her in the chest and falls.

The entire classroom becomes silent.

The teacher looks down at the paper.

Then slowly raises her eyes.

She scans the classroom.

Her eyes stop on Leon.

Leon freezes.

Jordan looks away.

Lucas tries not to react.

The teacher calmly picks up the paper.

**TEACHER:**

"Leon."

Leon slowly raises his eyes.

**LEON:**

"Yes, ma'am?"

The teacher gestures toward the door.

**TEACHER:**

"Come with me."

Leon stands.

The classroom watches.

Leon walks toward the door.

### TITLE REVEAL

Just as Leon reaches the doorway—

**CUT TO BLACK.**

A dramatic cinematic musical hit.

The title appears in elegant lettering:

## **AFTER THE BELL**

Hold the title briefly.

Then return to the story.

---

## SCENE 3 — THE CORRIDOR

**TIME:** Approximately 55 seconds

Leon follows the teacher through the magnificent corridor.

The school is still alive around them.

Students pass in both directions, talking quietly and heading toward their classes.

Leon glances back toward his classroom.

Jordan is standing at the doorway watching him.

Jordan gives Leon a look that says he is in serious trouble.

**JORDAN:**

"You're finished."

Lucas appears behind him.

**LUCAS:**

"Completely."

Leon shakes his head and continues walking.

The teacher leads him deeper into the administration wing.

As they turn a corner, Leon notices a senior school administrator named **DAVID** walking quickly toward them.

David is professionally dressed and carries a thick, dark examination folder.

He looks serious.

As David passes Leon, their eyes meet.

David briefly slows.

Something about his reaction feels unusual.

He immediately continues walking.

Leon watches him.

David reaches a restricted corridor.

He opens the door and disappears inside.

The door closes.

Leon slows down.

**LEON:**

"Who's that?"

The teacher doesn't stop.

**TEACHER:**

"Someone who works here."

Leon looks back at the closed door.

**LEON:**

"What was in that folder?"

The teacher finally looks at him.

**TEACHER:**

"Nothing you need to worry about."

Leon remains curious.

The teacher continues toward the principal's office.

Leon follows.

---

## SCENE 4 — THE PRINCIPAL'S OFFICE

**TIME:** Approximately 65 seconds

The teacher opens the door to an elegant principal's office.

Leon enters.

The room reflects the school's wealth and history.

A large wooden desk sits near the window.

Academic awards line the walls.

Old photographs show generations of successful students.

The **PRINCIPAL** sits behind the desk.

The teacher stands beside Leon.

**TEACHER:**

"He threw a folded paper across the classroom."

The principal looks directly at Leon.

**PRINCIPAL:**

"Did you?"

Leon doesn't try to deny it.

**LEON:**

"Yes, sir."

The principal studies him for a moment.

**PRINCIPAL:**

"Why?"

Leon gives a small shrug.

**LEON:**

"I thought it would be funny."

The principal remains serious.

**PRINCIPAL:**

"Don't let it happen again."

Leon nods.

**LEON:**

"It won't."

The teacher gives Leon a final look and leaves the office.

Leon turns toward the door.

Before he can leave, the door opens.

David enters.

He is carrying the same examination folder Leon saw in the corridor.

David walks directly to the principal's desk.

Leon notices the folder.

David places it on the desk.

The principal immediately becomes more serious.

David opens the folder.

Inside are confidential student examination records.

Leon accidentally catches a glimpse.

Several names appear.

Then one name catches his attention:

**MAYA.**

Leon stares for a moment.

The principal notices.

He immediately closes the folder.

Leon looks at him.

A brief silence.

---

## SCENE 5 — THE FIRST CLUE

**TIME:** Approximately 70 seconds

Leon remains near the doorway.

His curiosity has clearly been triggered.

He looks at the closed folder.

Then at David.

Then at the principal.

**LEON:**

"Sir…"

The principal looks at him.

**LEON:**

"What was in that folder?"

The principal's expression becomes controlled and serious.

**PRINCIPAL:**

"Student records."

Leon looks toward David.

**LEON:**

"Why did it look so important?"

David remains silent.

The principal answers firmly.

**PRINCIPAL:**

"Because it is private."

Leon nods slowly.

**LEON:**

"Understood."

He leaves the office.

The door closes behind him.

Inside the office, the principal looks at David.

**PRINCIPAL:**

"Those records stay here."

David nods.

**DAVID:**

"Of course."

Cut back to Leon walking through the corridor.

He is no longer thinking about the paper prank.

He is thinking about the name he saw.

**MAYA.**

Jordan and Lucas are waiting outside the classroom.

Jordan immediately approaches.

**JORDAN:**

"So? How bad was it?"

Leon looks toward the administration wing.

**LEON:**

"The paper wasn't the interesting part."

Lucas raises an eyebrow.

**LUCAS:**

"Then what was?"

Leon lowers his voice.

**LEON:**

"Examination records."

Jordan's expression changes.

**JORDAN:**

"Whose?"

Leon pauses.

**LEON:**

"Maya's."

Before either friend can respond, Leon notices David standing far down the corridor.

David is watching them.

Leon and David make eye contact.

David immediately turns and walks away.

Leon starts toward him.

Jordan catches his arm.

**JORDAN:**

"Where are you going?"

Leon looks toward the doorway David disappeared through.

**LEON:**

"To find out why he's watching us."

The school bell suddenly rings.

Students begin flooding into the corridor.

Leon loses sight of David.

The camera rises slowly above the moving students, revealing the enormous, beautiful corridor.

Everything looks normal.

But Leon's expression tells us something has changed.

**NARRATOR:**

"At Westbridge Academy, some secrets were buried behind closed doors."

Brief pause.

**NARRATOR:**

"And Leon had just found the first one."

**CUT TO BLACK.**

---

**END OF SCENES 1–5**
